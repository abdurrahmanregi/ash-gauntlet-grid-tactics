# Campaign

24 episodes and a coda. One battle each, except the coda. Scenes are a handful of lines. The march is: village, road, four animal seats, minister, lab, unifier twice.

Calendar label: Year of the Broken Calendar. Place names below are the replica’s.

Hollow Depths is not an episode. It opens after episode 13. Floors 1–4 then, 5–8 after 18, 9–12 after 22. Clearing a floor does not advance the episode pointer.

Sword-Two is in the party from the start of episode 1 (a village blade). Swallow joins in the talk after episode 2, before episode 3.

Episode 5’s Ash Warden uses the lord-hunt win condition but is **not** Issen-immune. Immunity starts at episode 11’s Cinder Bird and applies to every later named lord, including both Kurogane skins. Stair Captain (9) stays Issen-able so Oni-Wake and Issen can be tried on a named target.

## Twin revive (episode 7 only)

Two named beasts. If one is at 0 HP when the other takes an enemy turn, and the other is alive, the dead one returns at half max HP on an adjacent empty tile. No souls are paid back. Killing both on the player phase, before either enemy turn, is the clean clear. A revive can happen only once per beast per round.

## Gun nests (episode 12)

Three nests. MOV 0. Gun range 5. They do not drop stones. Destroying the nest is an ordinary kill for EXP and 10 souls. They never Issen-trigger because they are not using a melee Attack.

## Hollow Depths

Twelve single-room routs. No story. Floor level bands sit above the story enemies of the episode that unlocked them. Floor 12’s clear recruits Soot-Child at level 1, even if the party is much higher. He does not appear in story deployments before that clear.

## Scene rule

Each episode opens with at most eight lines and ends with at most four. No scene plays during the enemy phase. Recruit pitch happens on the deploy screen as one line under the portrait.
