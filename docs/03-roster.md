# Roster

17 allies. One weapon family each, forever. Level-1 numbers. Per-level gains are added to the level-1 stat, MOV never grows.

HP and SP gains are per level. ATK and DEF gains are per level.

Gun-Chief’s 8-salvo is eight damage rolls on one target, not eight targets. Swallow’s thunder and Coil-Champion’s thunder use the element-shot rule but reach range 4 in a line, not melee.

Dancer’s sleep and confuse last until the target takes two turns. A sleeping or confused unit does nothing on its turn, then the count drops. Damage removes sleep, not confuse.

Cross flash hits enemies in front and behind, up to 3 each way, same as a two-direction line.

## Who is mandatory

Kairo on every deploy. Everyone else is optional. Cap 8 including him.

## Starting kit

Episode 1 deploys Kairo (sword, no enhance) and Sword-Two (sword). Shio joins with a light sword and one herb in slot 1. Herbs heal 15 HP and are items, not skills.
