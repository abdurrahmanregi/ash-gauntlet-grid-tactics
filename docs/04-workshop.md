# Workshop

The only shop. No gold. Opens after episode 1, on the world map, before the next deploy.

## Stones

Four ranks, dropped by enemies, never bought.

| Rank | Typical source |
|---|---|
| Ash | Pawns, early maps |
| Bone | Bushi, spear maps |
| Cinder | Camp maps and factories |
| Void | Lords, Depths floors 9–12 |

A kill drops one stone of the family’s rank in addition to souls. Lords drop two Void. Nests drop none.

## Craft

A recipe is a named count of stones. Crafting spends them and adds the base item to the armory. The armory is shared. Equipping is done per unit on the deploy screen: one weapon, one armor, one accessory, two item slots.

Bodies refuse the wrong weapon family. Armor is universal. Accessories are universal in the starter kit.

## Enhance

Each crafted weapon, armor, or accessory enhances from +0 to +4. Cost is souls. A new tier replaces the previous bonus; it does not stack the listed ATK on top of itself. The table value is the total bonus at that tier.

Skill gates open at +2 or +4 and stay open at higher tiers. The skill spends SP like a personal skill and uses the unit’s action. It does not stack with a personal skill of the same name; the unit simply has it once.

## Starter recipes

Enough for the first maps. The rest of the book is a later pass.

| Item | Family | Stones | +1 | +2 | +3 | +4 | Skill gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ash blade | Sword | Ash ×3 | 200 souls, ATK +2 | 400, ATK +4 | 800, ATK +6 | 1200, ATK +8 | +4: 2-hit cut, 24 SP |
| Line spear | Spear | Ash ×2, Bone ×1 | 200, ATK +2 | 400, ATK +4 | 800, ATK +6 | 1200, ATK +8 | +4: line of 3, 18 SP |
| Nest gun | Gun | Bone ×2, Cinder ×1 | 300, ATK +2 | 600, ATK +4 | 1000, ATK +6 | 1600, ATK +8 | +4: 3-hit shot, 24 SP |
| Paper charm | Accessory | Ash ×1, Bone ×1 | 150, DEF +1 | 300, DEF +2 | — | — | +2: heal one, 10 SP. Cap +2 |
| Cedar coat | Armor | Ash ×2 | 150, DEF +1 | 300, DEF +2 | 500, DEF +3 | 800, DEF +4 | None |

Ash blade can be equipped by Kairo, Sword-Two, Stage-Man, Smith, Half-Blood. Not by light-sword users (Shio, Dancer) in this starter set. They keep their story light swords, which enhance on the Ash blade’s soul curve but never gain 2-hit cut. Their curve: same soul costs, ATK bonus half, rounded down ( +1 / +2 / +3 / +4 ).

Daggers, axes, and bows have no starter craft. Those bodies use the weapon they joined with (ATK +0, no skill) until a later recipe pass.

## Herbs

Not crafted in the starter book. Each episode prep grants two herbs if the party holds fewer than four. Herb: item, heal 15 HP, self or adjacent ally.

## What is deliberately missing

Named legendary weapons, element blades, Issen-chance accessories, and a 200-row stone matrix. Add those only after a battle can be played with this table.
